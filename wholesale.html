Partner With Us

Your customers deserve baked goods with a story. We bake in small batches right here in Hermiston, using quality ingredients and recipes refined by hand, so what lands in your pastry case tastes like it came from someone's kitchen, because it did.

We offer wholesale on everything you'll find on our menu (cookies, brownies, scones, muffins, and specialty desserts) as well as our packaged dry mixes for your retail shelf. Tell us what your customers love and we'll work out a lineup and schedule that fits.

Why partner with The Storybook Bakery?

  • Baked fresh, delivered local. No freezer trucks and no distributors, just short miles between our oven and your counter.
  • Flexible quantities. Order what your case actually sells, whether that's two dozen or ten.
  • Consistent quality. Small batches mean every delivery tastes like the first one.
  • Personal service. You'll always talk to the baker, not a call center.
