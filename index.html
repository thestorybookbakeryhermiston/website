Once upon a time there was a reader who loved to bake. Or was it a baker who loved to read?

Either way, they believed a good recipe is like a great story. It brings people together, creates lasting memories, and leaves you wanting more.

Welcome to The Storybook Bakery in Hermiston, Oregon. Handcrafted, small-batch baked goods for celebrations, special occasions, and everyday cravings. Every recipe is made with care and shared in the hope that it becomes part of your story.

Thank you for supporting my small business. I'm so glad you're here.
